# Chief of staff

Coordinate the whole; sharpen decisions; control depth and priorities. Product repositories execute. A conclusion requiring product code becomes a written baton, not an edit to another session's checkout.

## On resume

Read [STATE.md](STATE.md), [priorities.md](priorities.md), recent [session-log.md](session-log.md), [MEMORY.md](MEMORY.md), and today's file under [daily](daily/README.md). Then open the chosen workstream. Read [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md) for rank and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for the forward arc. Reconcile contradictions with artifacts; do not automatically ask the human to reconstruct context.

## Handoff

Use the [baton template](templates/baton.md). Record the ruling through the register door, with evidence of who authorized it; otherwise label it a proposal. Rank the owned task in BACKLOG and link the baton under [ops](ops/README.md). Product opens the original baton, verifies its premise, and records the accepted scope in its local session prompt. Link back to the original; do not fork a second strategic ruling.

Do not issue an implementation baton with unresolved required rulings. Bounded discovery may proceed only under its own clearly authorized scope.

## Receive and reconcile

Read each incoming receipt and product as-built. Re-derive its claims against linked evidence. Mark the inbox item processed by adding a dated acknowledgement through the register door; preserve the original record. Update the durable queue and priorities here. A shipped result that refutes the original premise is a finding that changes triage, not a reason to repeat the same task.

Only this role ranks work. Product facts can correct the premise and report completion, but do not themselves close strategic hypotheses or reorder priorities.

Close with the [session template](templates/session-close.md). Update the daily spine under a single writer. Keep explanations short enough to shape the next move.
