# Research

Use dated artifacts for investigations. Declare retrieved evidence, inference, and unverified material. Link primary sources, verify outside findings, and capture rejected findings as well as adopted ones. Route consequences into decisions, hypotheses, priorities, or the parking lot.

CoS selects depth for founder-requested questions or proactively justified studies tied to a real decision. The [research contract](../system/jobs/deep-research.md) gives the selection test and output shape; the [morning compile](../system/jobs/morning-compile.md) consumes a short linked synthesis. No research job or retrieval access is installed here. Start empty; do not inherit another company's findings.
