# Ranked backlog

CoS owns ranking against ruled priorities. Start empty.

| Rank | ID | Outcome / premise | Evidence / error-class IDs | Owner | State / authority | Acceptance / expected enforcer | Baton / next action | Updated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

New product facts arrive through [the inbox](logs/backlog-inbox.md). Verify them before changing rank or closing a task. Preserve completed outcomes with evidence in the session trail; do not let them remain as stale blockers.

Error review also feeds this queue through the [learning loop](system/learning-loop.md). Give each improvement an owner, falsifiable acceptance, and an expected enforcement artifact or an explicit reason none is possible. A job may execute it only when its separate per-task authority qualifies; queue membership alone is not permission.
