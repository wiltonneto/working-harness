# Morning compile contract

Read the [job contract and stop boundaries](README.md), supplied configuration, [working agreement](../WORKING_AGREEMENT.md), [STATE](../../STATE.md), and [CoS role](../../INSTRUCTIONS.md). Use the existing [daily template](../../templates/daily.md) rather than inventing another report format. No send or schedule is installed here.

Read the runner's job ledger, available evening review/research outputs, current priorities/queue/inbox, product status and receipts, recent rulings, and session closeouts. Recheck material claims at the linked artifacts. Missing output means unknown coverage; distinguish a failed job, a blocked lane, and a run whose outcome has not been established. Do not report a stale STARTED as success or a missing record as proof of no run.

Under one designated CoS writer, create the dated brief and today's spine. Respect live file ownership; if a conflict prevents writing the canonical daily file, leave a distinct output and report the handoff needed. Summarize:

- The most important consequence and where the company stands.
- Error classes reviewed: proposed improvements versus verified fixes; linked queue items and enforcers.
- Backlog changes: corrected stale premises, changed ranks with reasons, completed low-risk tasks, and outstanding blockers.
- Research: what was learned, contrary evidence, confidence/limits, and which decision the finding informs.
- Human rulings needed: recommendation, evidence, trade-off, and deadline. Keep implementation work already authorized out of this ruling queue.
- Next actions under delegation, failed/skipped/unknown lanes, actual usage/limits, and source freshness.

Keep the main brief short and consequence-first. Link detailed evidence instead of making the founder reconcile several overnight reports. A human verdict is never inferred from a green check. Artifact generation and external delivery are separate; no email, message, or publication is authorized by this prompt.
