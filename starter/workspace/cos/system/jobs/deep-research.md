# Evening deep-research contract

Read the [job contract and stop boundaries](README.md), supplied configuration, [working agreement](../WORKING_AGREEMENT.md), [STATE](../../STATE.md), current priorities/hypotheses, research requests, and recent work. CoS governs depth and study selection. No source access, usage, or provider authority is created by this prompt.

## Select work that earns uninterrupted depth

Take a founder-requested question with clear scope, or propose a proactive question tied to an active hypothesis, blocked decision, or material risk. Execute a proactive study only when the recorded delegation already covers that research method, sources, and allowance; otherwise leave it as a recommendation.

For each selected question, record:

1. Which decision or next action it could change, and the contrary finding that would change your recommendation.
2. Why it deserves depth rather than a short interactive lookup; what cannot be known yet.
3. Why it is suitable without live founder taste/judgment, and why the evidence will still be useful in the morning.
4. The expected information value versus time/usage, its freshness urgency, and any useful connection across workstreams.
5. The question's boundary, source/tool allowlist, output, allowance, and stopping condition.

Prioritize work that unblocks a real decision. Do not manufacture research to fill the night. Park low-value or judgment-dependent questions with a trigger. Narrow a study if the remaining allowance cannot support it; never silently substitute model recall for retrieval.

## Research and challenge

Use approved primary sources when available. Record retrieval time and provenance; distinguish retrieved evidence, inference, and unverified claims. Seek disconfirming evidence and keep rejected findings with reasons. Explain limitations in coverage and source access. If retrieval is unavailable, say so; an inference-only memo is not completed source research.

Do not contact people, sign into new services, purchase access, change providers, consume paid APIs, or gather unrelated private data. No production or product edits belong to this study. Research informs a ruling; it does not adopt/kill a thesis, change direction, or authorize implementation.

## Deliver for morning consumption

Write a distinct dated research artifact under `research/`: question and selection rationale; evidence and contrary evidence; synthesis; recommendation and confidence; what decision it informs; what remains unknown; usage and next test. Write a short dated pointer/output under `daily/` for morning compile, naming status and the few consequences that matter.

Route implications deliberately: an owned proposal through the backlog inbox, a hypothesis update for CoS/human review, or a parking trigger. Keep strategic rulings labeled as proposals until actually ruled. Avoid duplicate research by checking the existing question and recent artifacts first.
