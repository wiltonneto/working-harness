# Evening review contract

Read the [job contract and stop boundaries](README.md), the supplied configured job file, the [working agreement](../WORKING_AGREEMENT.md), [STATE](../../STATE.md), and [CoS role](../../INSTRUCTIONS.md). Do not proceed without the configured run ID, resource ownership, and bounded authority. No job is installed by this prompt.

## Read and review

Read priorities, BACKLOG and inbox, recent session/daily receipts, decisions, recent errors/open classes, and the current artifacts behind the highest-ranked items. Treat today's spine as context; do not overwrite it. Re-derive what shipped, what was superseded, what is still blocked, and which claims are unsupported. Do not derive tonight's agenda only from an old morning brief.

Apply the canonical [learning loop](../learning-loop.md) to error classes. Produce owned improvement candidates with related error IDs, evidence, expected enforcer, acceptance, and authority needed. Verify review findings before filing them; keep rejected findings with reasons. No new incidents means no invented error entries.

## Triage and execute only what qualifies

- Propose an evidence-backed queue order. If the job has explicit CoS ranking delegation, update BACKLOG within the already ruled priorities and record what changed and why. Otherwise leave the proposal for morning review.
- Inspect each proposed execution task against the per-task authority table. It must be in the approved CoS scope, reversible, bounded, verifiable, within remaining allowance, and independent of a human ruling. A clean branch or the word “easy” is not authority.
- Complete qualified work and verify it. Record exact files/revision/checks and file the completion facts through the register door. Do not leave an authorized completed fix invisible to the queue.
- Product work becomes a baton for an authorized product session. Changes to your own job contract, shared instructions, authority, or scheduler remain proposals even if they look like easy harness fixes.
- If a task does not qualify, state the missing decision/authority or conflict and move to another already permitted task. Do not turn the founder's absence into approval.

## Leave a morning input

Write one distinct dated output under `daily/`, with job/run ID, input revisions/freshness, classes reviewed, queue changes/proposals, completed tasks and evidence, usage, skipped/blocked lanes, rejected findings, and exact open actions with owners. Include research questions worth deeper work, ranked by the [research selection test](deep-research.md); a suggestion is not research completed.

Summarize consequences for morning compile: what improved, what still needs judgment, and what should happen next under existing authority. The runner records terminal state in the job ledger; do not imply success for a partial or blocked run. No email, post, deploy, or new schedule is part of this contract.
